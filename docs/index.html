About sns-auto-post

sns-auto-post is a social media management application designed for small and medium-sized businesses in Japan. It enables business owners to efficiently manage their social media presence across TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and Google Business Profile.

The application provides a unified interface for creating content, scheduling posts, and managing multiple store locations and their associated social media accounts.

Developed and operated by Night Safari Group.
