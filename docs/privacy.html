Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how sns-auto-post ("the App", "the Service", also referred to as "SNS Auto Post") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our social media management application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and Google Business Profile account identifiers (user ID, display name, page ID)
  • Authentication tokens provided through OAuth 2.0 authorization

2.2 Content Data

  • Videos and images you upload for posting
  • Captions, descriptions, and metadata associated with your content

2.3 Usage Data

  • Posting history and scheduling information
  • Service access logs

3. How We Use Your Information

sns-auto-post uses your information for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and connect to your social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Google Business Profile)
  • To publish content to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and Google Business Profile on your behalf
  • To manage posting schedules and content queues
  • To improve and maintain the Service

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your content is shared only with the social media platforms you authorize (e.g., TikTok) for the purpose of publishing your posts.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Authentication tokens are stored securely using encryption.
  • We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data.
  • Access to your data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion or disconnection, your authentication tokens and associated data will be deleted within 30 days.

7. Your Rights

  • Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your data at any time.
  • Revocation: You can revoke the Service's access to your social media accounts at any time through the respective platform's settings.

8. Third-Party Services

sns-auto-post integrates with the following third-party platforms. Your use of these platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Meta (Instagram/Facebook) Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the Service.
