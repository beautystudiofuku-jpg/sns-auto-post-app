Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how sns-auto-post ("the App", "the Service", also referred to as "SNS Auto Post"), operated by KOKO K.K. (also doing business as Night Safari Group), collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our social media management application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and Google Business Profile account identifiers (user ID, username, page ID, Instagram Business Account ID)
  • Authentication tokens (access tokens and refresh tokens) provided through OAuth 2.0 authorization
  • The list of Facebook Pages and connected Instagram Business accounts you manage, used to let you choose which destination to publish to

2.2 Content Data

  • Videos and images you upload for posting
  • Captions, descriptions, and metadata associated with your content

2.3 Usage Data

  • Posting history and scheduling information
  • Service access logs

3. How We Use Your Information

sns-auto-post uses your information for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and connect to your social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Google Business Profile)
  • To publish content to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and Google Business Profile on your behalf
  • To manage posting schedules and content queues
  • To improve and maintain the Service

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your content is shared only with the social media platforms you authorize (e.g., TikTok) for the purpose of publishing your posts.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Authentication tokens are stored securely using encryption.
  • We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data.
  • Access to your data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion or disconnection, your authentication tokens and associated data will be deleted within 30 days.

7. Your Rights

  • Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 8 below).
  • Revocation: You can revoke the Service's access to your social media accounts at any time through the respective platform's settings.

8. Data Deletion Instructions

You can request deletion of all data we hold about you in two ways:

  1. In-app deletion: Log in to sns-auto-post, open the store settings, and click "Disconnect" next to the social media account (TikTok / Instagram / Facebook / Google) you wish to remove. This immediately revokes the access token and deletes the encrypted token from our database. You may also click "Delete store" to remove all data associated with that store, including post history.
  2. Email request: Send a request to night.safari.group@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include the Facebook account name or Instagram username you used to connect. We will permanently delete the associated data within 30 days and confirm completion by email.

You can also revoke the App's access to your Meta (Facebook/Instagram) account at any time at Meta Business Integrations. When you remove the App there, Meta notifies us and we delete the associated tokens automatically.

9. Third-Party Services

sns-auto-post integrates with the following third-party platforms. Your use of these platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Meta (Instagram/Facebook) Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the Service.
